Sound belongs to the room.

Sora Siam is designed as a food-and-sound destination. Music is part of the experience, while Thai-Japanese dining, cocktails and wine remain central to the evening.

Live music

Live sets are planned for selected nights. Each confirmed event will receive its own date, artist information and page when the opening program is ready.

Hi-res listening

Between live programs, Sora Siam will operate as a listening room. Hi-res describes the in-room playback experience, not the compressed audio delivered by this website.

Dinner, drinks and sound in one place.

The planned evening brings together Thai signatures, fresh sashimi, dry-aged fish and steak, cocktails, selected wine and a carefully paced sound program.

Music questions, answered plainly.

Will Sora Siam have live music?

Yes. Live music is planned for selected nights. The first artists and dates have not yet been announced.

Will there be live music every night?

No nightly schedule has been announced. Sora Siam plans selected live sets, with hi-res listening between them.

What does hi-res listening mean at Sora Siam?

It refers to the restaurant's high-quality listening program between live sets. Equipment brands and technical specifications are still to be confirmed.

Where is the Sora Siam listening room?

It is part of the forthcoming Sora Siam restaurant at 378 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10016, in Kips Bay, Manhattan.
