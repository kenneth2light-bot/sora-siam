01 The room

Sound with a sense of place.

03 Questions

After Dark, answered plainly.

Will SORA After Dark have live music?+

Yes, on selected nights. No nightly schedule, artist lineup or performance calendar has been announced.

Is the entire restaurant a live music venue?+

No. The upstairs room is centered on dining. SORA After Dark is the downstairs room for cocktails, selected live sets and high-fidelity listening.

What happens when there is no live set?+

The planned downstairs program shifts to high-fidelity listening and cocktails between live nights.

Are tickets or reservations available?+

Not yet. Any confirmed event will receive its own date, performer details and access link when the program is approved.
