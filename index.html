What kind of restaurant is Sora Siam?+

Sora Siam is a forthcoming Thai-Japanese restaurant and listening room built around Thai signatures, Japanese knife work, fresh sashimi, dry-aged fish and steak, cocktails, wine and sound.

Sora Siam is coming to 378 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10016, in Kips Bay, Manhattan.
