ABOUT SORA SIAM

A restaurant that changes pace after dinner.

Thai flavor, Japanese precision and patient aging upstairs. Cocktails, selected live sets and high-fidelity listening downstairs.

Join opening list ↘

01 Our direction

Siam in spirit. Sora in atmosphere.

Sora Siam starts with the table: bright herbs, chile, smoke, careful knife work, fresh raw preparations and the depth that comes from time. The restaurant then opens into a second rhythm downstairs, where cocktails and sound carry the night forward.

The goal is not fusion for novelty. It is a coherent evening in which food, drink, room and music feel considered together.

01

Warm, not formal

Low light, an open table and serious preparation without making the guest perform expertise.

02

Precise, not precious

Clean knife work, exact temperature and details that improve the meal without requiring explanation.

03

Alive after dark

Warm sound and selected live sets add energy while the dining room remains a restaurant first.

02 The rooms

One address, two distinct chapters.

Upstairs is for Thai-Japanese dining, sashimi, aged cuts and the bar. Downstairs is SORA After Dark for cocktails, selected live sets and high-fidelity listening.

03 Questions

Sora Siam, answered plainly.

What is Sora Siam?+

Sora Siam is a forthcoming Thai-Japanese restaurant with an upstairs dining room and a downstairs After Dark room in Kips Bay.

What defines the food?+

Thai signatures, Japanese technique, fresh sashimi, dry-aged fish and steak form the confirmed direction. The final menu is still in development.

Where is Sora Siam?+
