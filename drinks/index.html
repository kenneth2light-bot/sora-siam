01 The bar

Choose by mood, not by expertise.

The menu will make room for a clean classic, a house drink with a more particular point of view, something lower in alcohol, or no alcohol at all. The same bar serves the upstairs dining room and the downstairs After Dark room.

03 At the table

Made for the whole menu arc.

Bright and dry with sashimi. Aromatic with Thai herbs and chile. Structured enough for smoke and aged cuts. The final list will be edited around the food, not assembled beside it.

04 Questions

Drinks, answered plainly.

What drinks will Sora Siam serve?+

The confirmed direction includes signature cocktails, familiar classics, sake, selected wines, and low-ABV and zero-proof drinks.

Will there be approachable cocktails?+

Yes. The program is designed for both guests who want a recognizable classic and guests looking for a more distinctive house drink.

Are the cocktail names and prices published?+

Not yet. Final drinks, ingredients, brands, prices and availability will be published with the approved opening menu.
