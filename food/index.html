01 Point of view

Not two menus. One conversation.

Sora Siam moves between Thai aromatics and Japanese restraint without dividing the table into separate cuisines. The menu is designed to travel from raw and chilled preparations through smoke, rice, noodles and the grill.

03 Aging

Time is part of the ingredient list.

04 Questions

Food, answered plainly.

Is Sora Siam a Thai restaurant or a Japanese restaurant?+

Sora Siam is one Thai-Japanese restaurant, not two separate concepts. Thai flavor, Japanese precision and a shared focus on exact preparation shape the menu.

Will Sora Siam serve sashimi?+

Yes. Fresh sashimi and raw preparations are part of the confirmed food direction. Exact fish and dishes have not been announced.

What is being dry-aged?+

Dry-aged fish and dry-aged steak are both part of the planned program. Exact cuts, species and aging windows will be published with the approved menu.

Is the full menu available now?+

Not yet. The categories shown here describe the opening direction; final dishes, names, prices and availability may change before opening.
