A focused Asian dining point of view.

Sora Siam is not presenting two separate menus. Thai flavor, Japanese precision, controlled aging and a bar built for the food belong to one restaurant experience.

Thai flavor. Japanese precision.

Thai specialties bring heat, herbs and smoke. Japanese knife work and exact temperature guide the sashimi program. Final dishes will be named only when the opening menu is complete.

Time is part of the ingredient list.

Dry-aged fish and dry-aged steak are confirmed parts of the Sora Siam food program. Aging is used to develop texture, aroma and umami, with exact cuts and timelines still to be announced.

Food questions, answered plainly.

Is Sora Siam a Thai restaurant or a Japanese restaurant?

Sora Siam is a Thai-Japanese restaurant. Thai signatures meet Japanese technique, fresh sashimi and a shared focus on precise preparation.

What kind of Asian food will Sora Siam serve?

The confirmed direction includes Thai signatures, fresh sashimi, Thai-Japanese fusion, dry-aged fish and dry-aged steak. Exact dishes will be announced with the opening menu.

Will Sora Siam serve sashimi?

Fresh sashimi is a confirmed part of the food program. Exact fish and preparations have not yet been announced.

Is the Sora Siam menu available now?

Not yet. The final menu, prices and opening date will be published only after they are confirmed.
