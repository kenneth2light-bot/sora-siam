Last updated August 16, 2026

Current preview

Opening-list enrollment is not active on this preview. No email field is shown, and Sora Siam is not currently collecting or storing visitor email addresses through this site.

Before enrollment opens

This notice will be updated before Sora Siam begins collecting email addresses. Enrollment will be limited to opening news, menu previews, music announcements and reservation access, with an unsubscribe option in every message.

Site delivery

The hosting provider may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, browser type and request time, to deliver and protect the site. Sora Siam does not currently add advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies to this preview.
